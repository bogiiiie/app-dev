# Forrest Gump (1994)

**Forrest Gump** is a cinematic masterpiece that chronicles the extraordinary life of a kind-hearted Alabama man with a low IQ who unintentionally witnesses and influences some of the most defining historical events of the 20th century. 

---

## 🎬 Synopsis

The story unfolds as Forrest Gump waits at a bus stop, sharing his remarkable life journey with strangers. Despite his cognitive limitations and physical challenges as a child, Forrest lives a life that anyone would envy. Guided by his mother's simple wisdom—*"Life is like a box of chocolates, you never know what you're gonna get"*—Forrest breezes through decades of American history, operating entirely on pure innocence and an unwavering devotion to his childhood sweetheart, Jenny.

---

## 📋 Movie Details

* **Director:** Robert Zemeckis
* **Screenplay:** Eric Roth (Based on the novel by Winston Groom)
* **Release Date:** July 6, 1994
* **Runtime:** 142 minutes
* **Genre:** Drama / Romance / Comedy-Drama

---

## 🌟 Key Story Arcs & Historical Milestones

* **Childhood Challenges:** Overcoming leg braces and discovering a legendary running ability.
* **College Football:** Becoming an All-American football star and meeting President John F. Kennedy.
* **Vietnam War:** Serving in the U.S. Army, saving his platoon, and receiving the Medal of Honor.
* **Ping-Pong Diplomacy:** Representing the United States in historic matches against China.
* **The Shrimping Business:** Founding the Bubba Gump Shrimp Company and becoming an accidental millionaire.
* **The Cross-Country Run:** Inspiring the nation by running across America for over three years.

---

## 🎭 Main Characters

* **Tom Hanks** as *Forrest Gump* – The sweet, fiercely loyal protagonist.
* **Robin Wright** as *Jenny Curran* – Forrest’s lifelong love who battles her own demons.
* **Gary Sinise** as *Lieutenant Dan Taylor* – Forrest's cynical but honorable military commander.
* **Mykelti Williamson** as *Benjamin Buford "Bubba" Blue* – Forrest's best friend in the army who loves shrimp.
* **Sally Field** as *Mrs. Gump* – Forrest's fiercely protective and loving mother.

---

## 🏆 Key Accolades

The film won **six Academy Awards** in 1995, including:
1. Best Picture
2. Best Actor in a Leading Role (Tom Hanks)
3. Best Director (Robert Zemeckis)
4. Best Writing, Screenplay Based on Material Previously Produced or Published
5. Best Film Editing
6. Best Visual Effects
